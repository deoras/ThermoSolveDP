About Qt / PySide6 (LGPL v3)
----------------------------
Qt and PySide6 are used unmodified, as separate shared libraries. You may
replace them with your own build of the same libraries: in the source
package, install a different PySide6 version into the .venv folder; in the
ready-made apps, the Qt libraries are ordinary files inside the application
folder (ThermoSolveDP.app/Contents/Frameworks on macOS, the _internal folder
on Windows). The full LGPL v3 text is at https://www.gnu.org/licenses/lgpl-3.0.txt
and the Qt source code is available from https://download.qt.io and
https://code.qt.io.

Thermodynamic data
------------------
Fluid properties are calculated by CoolProp (Bell et al., Ind. Eng. Chem.
Res. 2014, 53, 2498-2508). Standard enthalpies of formation used by the
reactor are from the NIST Chemistry WebBook / NIST-JANAF tables.
